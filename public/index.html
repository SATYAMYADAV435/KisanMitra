    🏺 Test 1: Soil Feel & Ribbon Test (मिट्टी का रिबन परीक्षण) DIY Field Test
    💡 Take a handful of moist soil and roll it between your palms into a ribbon/pencil.
    🟤
    Black Cotton Soil (मध्यम काळी)
    Forms smooth flexible ribbon, high water hold
    🌾
    Fertile Loam (गाळाची दोमट)
    Rolls then crumbles, most fertile & balanced
    🏖️
    Sandy / Light (हलकी वाळूमिश्रित)
    Gritty feel, won't form ribbon, fast draining
    🔴
    Red / Gravelly (लाल मुरुमाची)
    Pebbly, good aeration, needs frequent manure

    Slow / Waterlogging (पाणी साचते)

    Very Fast (अतिजलद निचरा)
